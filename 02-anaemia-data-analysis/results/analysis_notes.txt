PROJECT 2: ANAEMIA DATA ANALYSIS

Dataset: NFHS-5 (2019-21), State/UT-wise anaemia prevalence.
Source: Government of India, Lok Sabha Unstarred Question No. 1953, answered on 02 August 2024.
Source URL: https://sansad.in/getFile/loksabhaquestions/annex/182/AU1953_osmtyG.pdf

Analysis includes descriptive statistics, data quality checks, state-wise comparisons, visualisations and exploratory correlations.

The average across States/UTs is unweighted and should not be interpreted as national prevalence.

This is an ecological descriptive analysis. It does not establish causation or provide individual diagnosis or clinical advice.
